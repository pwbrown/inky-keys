# Credits

Third-party content bundled with Inky Keys, with its source and license (REQUIREMENTS §12).

## Word list

- **What:** English words in `src/content/en/words.json`, generated by `scripts/build-words.ts`.
- **Source:** [SCOWL](http://wordlist.aspell.net/) (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists) by Kevin Atkinson. Size levels 10, 20, 35, 40 and 50 are used, via the [`wordlist-english`](https://github.com/jacksonrayhamilton/wordlist-english) npm package (MIT).
- **License:** the SCOWL license, which permits use and redistribution with its copyright notice. The full notice is in [`licenses/SCOWL.txt`](licenses/SCOWL.txt).
- **Changes:** filtered to lowercase a–z words of 2–12 letters, with offensive words removed using the blocklist below.

## French word list

- **What:** French words in `src/content/fr/words.json`, generated by `scripts/build-words.ts`. **This file alone is under CC BY-SA 4.0** (share-alike); the game's code stays MIT.
- **Source:** [Lexique 3.83](http://www.lexique.org/) by Boris New, Christophe Pallier and contributors. Please cite: New, B., Pallier, C., Brysbaert, M., & Ferrand, L. (2004). Lexique 2: A new French lexical database. *Behavior Research Methods, Instruments, & Computers*, 36(3), 516–524.
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The full text is in [`licenses/Lexique-CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`](licenses/Lexique-CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt).
- **Changes:** word forms with a book or film-subtitle frequency, ranked by the average of the two and split into five tiers by rank; kept only lowercase words of 2–12 letters using a–z and `à â ç é è ê ë î ï ô ù û ü`; left out words with `œ`, `æ` or `ÿ` (including those Lexique spells with `oe`); offensive words removed with the French blocklist below.

## Spanish word list

- **What:** Spanish words in `src/content/es/words.json`, generated by `scripts/build-words.ts`. **This file alone is under CC BY-SA 4.0** (share-alike); the game's code stays MIT.
- **Source:** [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) by Hermit Dave (`content/2018/es/es_full.txt`), word counts from the [OpenSubtitles 2018](https://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php) corpus ([opensubtitles.org](https://www.opensubtitles.org/)), via OPUS: P. Lison and J. Tiedemann (2016), *OpenSubtitles2016: Extracting Large Parallel Corpora from Movie and TV Subtitles*, LREC 2016.
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The full text is in [`licenses/FrequencyWords-CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`](licenses/FrequencyWords-CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt).
- **Changes:** ranked by count (words seen at least 20 times) and split into five tiers by rank; kept only lowercase words of 2–12 letters using a–z and `á é í ó ú ü ñ` that the [RLA-ES](https://github.com/sbosio/rla-es) Hunspell dictionary by Santiago Bosio accepts in lowercase (via [`wooorm/dictionaries`](https://github.com/wooorm/dictionaries) `es`, GPL-3.0 / LGPL-3.0 / MPL-1.1, checked with [`nspell`](https://github.com/wooorm/nspell), MIT), which drops names, English words and typos; the dictionary itself isn't bundled; offensive words removed with the Spanish blocklist below.

## Blocklist

- **What:** terms in `src/content/en/blocklist.json`, `src/content/fr/blocklist.json` and `src/content/es/blocklist.json`, used to filter the word list and every generated string.
- **Source:** [List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words](https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words) (LDNOOBW), via the [`naughty-words`](https://github.com/LDNOOBW/naughty-words-js) npm package (its English, French and Spanish lists).
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **Changes:** multi-word phrases removed; extra terms added from a kid-friendliness review (sexual and reproductive words, identity words, toilet humor, crude insults and slurs, drugs, alcohol and tobacco, and graphic violence); and an allowlist of harmless words the matching rules caught by mistake. The review terms are grouped in `src/content/contentFilter.ts`, where each group is allowed back only at a looser word-filter level; the allowlist is in `scripts/build-words.ts`.

## Topic word lists (optional download)

- **What:** words downloaded on request in the Word lists screen. Nothing is bundled.
- **Source:** the plain text of the best-matching [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/) article (or [French Wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/) for French words, [Spanish Wikipedia](https://es.wikipedia.org/) for Spanish), fetched through the public MediaWiki API (no key). Article text is © Wikipedia contributors under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- **Use:** only individual dictionary words are kept (no sentences or passages), ranked by how much more often they appear in the article than in everyday English, and run through the blocklist. The article text itself is discarded. (Before 2.15.0, lists came from the [Datamuse API](https://www.datamuse.com/api/); lists downloaded then still work.)

## Fonts

- **What:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (target text, menus) and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono (Type Case key caps), Latin subsets, bundled via `src/fonts.ts`.
- **Source:** the [Braille Institute](https://www.brailleinstitute.org/freefont/) and the Atkinson Hyperlegible Next Project Authors, via the [`@fontsource/atkinson-hyperlegible-next`](https://fontsource.org/fonts/atkinson-hyperlegible-next) and [`@fontsource/atkinson-hyperlegible-mono`](https://fontsource.org/fonts/atkinson-hyperlegible-mono) npm packages.
- **License:** SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full texts are in [`licenses/AtkinsonHyperlegibleNext-OFL.txt`](licenses/AtkinsonHyperlegibleNext-OFL.txt) and [`licenses/AtkinsonHyperlegibleMono-OFL.txt`](licenses/AtkinsonHyperlegibleMono-OFL.txt).
- **Why:** it was designed so look-alike characters (l I 1 |, 0 O) are easy to tell apart.

- **What:** [jsQR](https://github.com/cozmo/jsQR) 1.4.0 by Cosmo Wolfe, which reads QR codes from the camera for Play together's **Scan QR code**. It's bundled from the `jsqr` npm package and loaded only when a player presses Scan.
- **License:** Apache License 2.0. The full text is in [`licenses/jsQR-Apache-2.0.txt`](licenses/jsQR-Apache-2.0.txt).
- **Changes:** none. (The QR codes the game shows are drawn by its own encoder, `src/core/qr.ts`, which follows the structure of [Project Nayuki's QR Code generator](https://www.nayuki.io/page/qr-code-generator-library) (MIT).)
